How to use

Click any heart to load your own video or image — it will fill the heart shape. The pink gem outline from the PNG overlay stays on top, so your media shows through the heart window.

To swap files in the final HTML, replace the src paths at the top of the file:

Background.jpg — your frost/nature background
Image_border.PNG — the gold scrollwork title frame
4_individual_Heart_shaped_video_holders.PNG — the 4 gem hearts overlay

For real videos, replace the placeholder <div> elements with <video src="your.mp4" autoplay muted loop playsinline></video> inside each .media-cell.
